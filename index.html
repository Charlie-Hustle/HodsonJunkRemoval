Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you book.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the amount and type of material, labor required, access to the items, disposal fees, and travel. You receive an estimate before the work begins.

Can I send photos for an estimate?

Yes. Photos are often the fastest way to understand the size of the job. Send them by email to charles@hodsonjunkremoval.com.

Do you offer single-item pickup?

Yes. Hodson Junk Removal can help with bulky individual items as well as larger cleanouts and debris removal projects.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. If the items are accessible and the details are confirmed beforehand, some pickups can be completed without you being on-site.

How far do you travel?

Service availability depends on the size and scope of the project. Contact us with the job location and details, and we will let you know whether we can help.
